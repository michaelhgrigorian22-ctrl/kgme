# The station (CDH-0009; cycle 2 under CDH-0012, cycle 3 and the rubric checks under CDH-0015 and CDH-0014)

A second wall layout: the ship, its rooms and the crew at work, drawn dark and on theme. Not a canvas artboard (a rendered scene is script-built, which the `.dc.html` editor forbids): one hand-built page.

- `index.html` — the page. Serve `docs/design/cockpit-v0/` over HTTP (`python3 -m http.server 8080` from that folder) and open `/station/`. It reads the cast from `../chibi/*.svg` at load and replays `sample-events.jsonl` at ×20, looping; `p` or the HUD's control pauses the replay (ux-guidelines 108), and under reduced motion it starts paused. Two read-only query parameters exist for checks: `?speed=N` (replay rate, 1–60, default 20) and `?at=HH:MM:SS` (start the replay at that clock on 2026-10-08).
- `sample-events.jsonl` — 40 ledger lines in ADR-019 event v1 shape, one JSON object per line, derived from the samples boards 3, 5 and 11 carry (2026-10-08, 19:40 to 19:52). Ids are ULID-shaped; raw bodies never appear. The sample holds no halt note and no approval; the halt path is shown with a three-line synthetic sample (a halt, a bookkeeping note, an approval; all 43 lines pass `validate_event`) kept under `logs/scratch/design-review/cdh-0015/` for the review, never here.
- The page loads the pinned `three@0.182.0` package from jsDelivr through an import map (`three.module.min.js` and `examples/jsm/`) and the repository's own files, nothing else: no font is fetched (the type is the system monospace, AUDIT-CDH-0011 S6). The cockpit itself may load nothing remote (ADR-017); HANDOFF-0044 proposes vendoring the module as plain text under `cockpit/static/vendor/` when the station leaves the design track.
- Rooms are KGME places: Bridge (you), Workshop (`claude-code.*`), Review chamber (`kgme.review`, `arbiter.*`), Airlock (`kgme.guard`), Archive (`kgme.*`), Dock (shells, the stranger), Engine room (phase 2, dashed). The sprite mapping is a copy of `kgme.toml [sprites]`, first match wins.
- Words first, from one snapshot (AUDIT-CDH-0011 S1): every 250 ms the page builds one textual snapshot — clock, ledger line, session, harnesses, the hull (`nominal`, or `HALT · <the note's key>` in the HUD and `HALT · <the note>` in the wall's header while a halt note governs a run (CDH-0017 CW19): until an intent, outcome or approval follows it, the same rule the lifecycle uses; a later bookkeeping note does not clear it, AUDIT-CDH-0013 F1), the tray summary, each room's state line, each run's row, and the last event of the bridge (Michou's note or approval), the airlock (the guard's last refusal) and the review chamber (the arbiter's last ruling), which stay as text after their bubbles fade. A bubble hangs below its crew member's tag, so it never covers a room's chip (CDH-0017 CW16); the deck is framed so the top rooms' chips sit clear of the HUD at 1440. The HUD, the room chips, the pane and the wall all read that snapshot; the lights mirror it and never replace it. The count's changes are announced once, atomically, by one visually hidden `role="status"` region reading `active runs: …` (ux-guidelines 118); nothing else in the page is live, and no change moves focus. Keys: `1`–`7` follow a room, `Esc` the whole deck, `a` the runs pane, `c` calm (drops to the wall: the HUD stays, the deck, frame, portraits and micro labels go, every fact remains), `p` pause, `r` restarts the replay. Single-character keys never act while focus is in a text field (WCAG 2.1.4; the page has none, the guard is in the handler), and only while the page itself has focus. Reduced motion removes bloom, walks and fades, starts the replay paused, and is observed while the page runs. The pane and the wall sit below the measured HUD (`--hudtop`), so a focused row is never under it (ux-guidelines 100); the stage is never sized while hidden and is sized again when calm lifts (AUDIT-CDH-0013 F2).
- Rows are keyed by run and updated in place (S4): a focused row survives every refresh; a row's name is its visible text (mapping, run, word, quiet, elapsed, event, chip). The room buttons stay in the accessibility tree with their state in their name; only the canvas and the portraits are marked decorative, so a crew member's tag and its last bubble (a fixture's last summary) stay readable outside calm (S3, AUDIT-CDH-0013 F6). Every muted line of a focused row is `#7a8d9b` (S5). Every button is at least 24 × 24 CSS px (ux-guidelines 104).
- Lifecycle words, as on board 11 (S2, S7): working · waiting (the newest intent or outcome is an intent, with no outcome yet, whatever its parent) · transient (the newest outcome is class transient, or an exhausted retry that names a time) · blocked (the newest outcome is class blocking, or a blocking note that no intent, outcome or approval has followed) · quiet (a working run with no event for longer than its median gap between intents — the ordinary median, the mean of the two middle gaps when their count is even — and never less than three minutes; with fewer than two intents, three minutes). A job run closes with the outcome of its run intent; a hook session stays open until a `session:end` note.

Review copy: the private artifact "KGME Station" (Cowork's), republished from this folder.

Against `control/review/design-reference/ui-ux-pro-max/data/stacks/threejs.csv` rows 1–9 (CDH-0014): 1 the package is pinned (`three@0.182.0`, core and addons on one release; the rubric's "current" 0.185.1 is a version choice for HANDOFF-0044); 2 no capsule shapes; 3 no OrbitControls: the camera is a fixed isometric orthographic view, by design; 4 the drag handler pans the look-at target in the ground plane, so both axes respond (the row's defect, a vertical drag ignored, does not arise; there is no orbit to get wrong); 5 one ESM graph through the import map; 6 one `WebGLRenderer` for the page's life (the `CSS2DRenderer` is DOM, not a GL context); 7 `Math.min(devicePixelRatio, 2)`; 8 not applied: the canvas composites over nothing (`#stage` is the opaque ground colour and the composer's targets are opaque), so the scene keeps its own background and the row's purpose does not arise; 9 the orthographic bounds follow the aspect on every resize and `updateProjectionMatrix()` is called.
