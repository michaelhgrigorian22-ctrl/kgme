what a sprite may do

Blink, work its own tool, glance, sag, burn, wear a halo or lie in chalk. Up to 2 authored key poses use interpolated transforms. Only the selected visible detail animates. Calm removes artwork without losing actor, lifecycle, class or event text. Hidden, offscreen and reduced-motion previews do no animation work.

what a sprite is

One editable SVG under chibi/, with 6 material roles and restrained cel shading. Smooth source geometry replaces the retired 24 × 24 grid and 3 KB cap. Phosphor stays in the timeline; sticker is confined to detail headers and the illustrated Phase 2 selector. The 22 px event rows, keyboard map and 3 strike boxes stay unchanged.
