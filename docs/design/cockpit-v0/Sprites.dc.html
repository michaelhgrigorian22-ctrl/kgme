Sprites — the cast and its modes

One sprite per actor, 24 × 24 pixels, original line work, two palettes from one drawing. A sprite's mode repeats the class chip on its row and nothing else, so it is noise by the rule: c hides the whole cast, reduced motion freezes it to one pose. Each is one SVG under cockpit/sprites/; a new harness is a file and a line in kgme.toml [sprites], and until then it is the stranger.

Matched on actor, first rule wins. A Kimi harness is the stranger until you give it a face.
what a sprite may do

Blink, shake a tool, hold up a glyph, melt, burn, sit down, lie in chalk. Two frames, one accent colour: the row's class. It may never show a fact the row does not show in text, so the cast can vanish under c and nothing is lost. Under prefers-reduced-motion every sprite is frame A, still: on fire, but still.

what a sprite is

One file, cockpit/sprites/builder.svg, under 3 KB, 24 × 24, six fills (b garment, s face, h hat and tool, p print, d dark, a accent), seven groups for the modes. Original line work; no stock, no game, no named character. Reviewed like code.
