# Handoff 0046 (proposed) — `kgme dream`: the week's ledger read out of band, instincts proposed, nothing adopted by itself

## Why
Michou, 2026-10-13, on the Anthropic talk's "dreaming" (out-of-band review of transcripts that updates memory) and on self-prompting loops: "this whole project would be way easier if i could actually create one of these autonomous prompting loops." The loop is HANDOFF-0043. The dreaming half has its substrate already: `logs/ledger/` is the transcript store, `docs/audit/` and `logs/scratch/` hold the review history. What is missing is the reader of that record and the gate on what it may change. ECC's `continuous-learning-v2` (reviewed in `memory/agent/2026-10-13-external-repos-review.md`) is the shape: observations → observer → atomic instincts with confidence and evidence → promotion gate. KGME's differences are deliberate: the record stays in the repo, the input is the ledger's redacted summaries and never raw prompts, and promotion is a handoff Michou adopts.

## What
1. **`kgme dream [--since YYYY-MM-DD] [--dry-run]`.** A job under ADR-013 (profile row `kgme-dream`, read-only, no network, Tier 2 at most). Inputs: `logs/ledger/` since the last dream (or seven days), every `docs/audit/AUDIT-*` and disposition in `logs/scratch/` from the window, the current readers, `CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/settings.json`. Model: the reviewer (`kgme.toml [models] reviewer`, Codex read-only) at first; a local model when router v0 exists and the job's tier allows.
2. **The prompt is the retro rubric.** `tools/review/dream-prompt.md`, fixed like `prompt.md`, asks for candidates in the seven classes of `retro` (`mattpocock/skills`, vendored by HANDOFF-0044): navigation pointers, automated checks that would have caught a mistake, standards (mechanical → a check, judgement → a reader line), oversized steering, tool economy, no-op instructions, missing information access; plus KGME's own: root causes that recurred across handoffs, halts and what closed them, guard refusals that were right and wrong.
3. **Outputs are notes and one proposal.** Each candidate becomes an instinct note in `memory/agent/`: `type: note`, `source: inferred`, `author: kgme`, `sources` naming the ledger ids and audit ids it rests on, tags `instinct`, `confidence:0.3..0.9`, `domain:<class>`, body "Trigger / Action / Evidence". The job then writes one proposed handoff in `docs/handoff/proposed/` listing the instincts at confidence ≥ 0.7 with the exact diffs they imply for readers, `CLAUDE.md` or the guard, `from: kgme`, `status: proposed`. It never edits a reader, an ADR, `CLAUDE.md`, the guard or the schema; adoption is Michou moving the file, as for every proposal.
4. **Promotion and decay.** An instinct seen again in a later dream raises its confidence by 0.1 (recorded as a new note that `supersedes` the old); one not seen for four dreams is set `status: archived`. Nothing is deleted.
5. **The ledger shows it.** One `intent`, one `outcome` per dream, `job: dream`, payload the list of note ids; a dream that produces no candidate is a success with an empty list. The cockpit shows it as any job.
6. **Schedule.** By hand (`kgme dream`) until the Phase 3 heartbeat exists; then Sunday 17:00 PT, an hour before the weekly review, so the review reads the dream's proposal.

## Outputs
`kgme/dream.py` (effects: `run_dream`; logic: `select_window`, `parse_candidates`, `confidence_update`, pure), `kgme/cli.py` (`dream`), `tools/review/dream-prompt.md`, `tools/policy/profiles/kgme-dream.toml`, `memory/templates/instinct.md`, `tests/test_dream.py` (a synthetic ledger with one recurring root cause yields one instinct at 0.7 and one proposal; a second run raises it to 0.8 with `supersedes`; an empty window yields a success with no notes; a note the job tries to write outside `memory/agent/` or `docs/handoff/proposed/` is refused by the guard), `jobs/dream/job.md`, canary rows for the profile.

## Acceptance test (under ten minutes)
`kgme dream --since <a week with one real halt>` runs read-only, writes at least one instinct whose `sources` name the audit of that halt, writes one proposal whose diffs reference real reader lines, appends the pair of ledger events, and touches nothing else (`git status --porcelain` shows only `memory/agent/` and `docs/handoff/proposed/`). `kgme dream --dry-run` prints the candidates and writes nothing.
