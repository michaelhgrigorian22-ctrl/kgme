# Handoff 0049 (proposed) — One home for KGME: `~/Projects/KGME/` with the brain, design and work side by side; folders labelled by role; coherence checked by lint

## Why
Michou, 2026-10-16: "i think its about time to rid the automate folder of any visible clutter and make the pathing as intuitive as humanly possible with improved labelling and reverification that everything is coherent", and "that should include some merged mass folder with design and backend that cleanly delegates and separates and holds the central brain and command files because right now they are fragmented."

Cowork's audit of the tree on 2026-10-16 (read-only):
- **Fragmented at the machine level.** `~/Projects/Automate` (branch `main`) and `~/Projects/Automate-design` (a worktree on `design`) are loose siblings among Michou's other projects; `crew` (HANDOFF-0048) would add a third root, `~/Projects/kgme-work/`.
- **One mislabelled folder.** `tools/` holds the rules agents run under (the hook scripts, the git hook, the policy profiles, the review prompts, the schema entry point), while `CLAUDE.md` describes it as "MCP servers, one per capability."
- **Handoffs mix states.** `docs/handoff/` holds 26 finished handoffs, 1 abandoned one and the 1 open one (0034); `docs/handoff/proposed/` holds 14 live proposals (before this one) next to 6 superseded or abandoned ones.
- **Broken links in the record.** Eight handoffs name a report path with a predicted date that never existed: 0025 and 0026 (`2026-09-15-packaging.md`, the report is `2026-09-14-packaging.md`), 0027 and 0028 (`2026-09-16-permissions-and-hooks.md` → `2026-09-15-…`), 0029 (`2026-09-17-review-wrapper.md` → `2026-09-16-…`), 0031 (`2026-09-18-validator.md` → `2026-09-20-…`), 0032 (`2026-09-19-canaries.md` → `2026-09-20-…`), 0033 and 0034 (`2026-09-20-loop-0.md` → `2026-10-08-loop-0.md`); `docs/corrections.md` names `2026-09-10-machine-audit.md` (the report is `2026-09-09-machine-audit.md`); ADR-019 names `tools/ledger` (the ledger writer is `kgme/ledger.py`). The other 63 unresolved paths in live documents are future outputs of open or proposed handoffs, examples, design-branch files, or `memory/private` (absent by design).
- **A stale map.** `CLAUDE.md` lists `router/` and `launchd/` (not built), describes `tools/` wrongly, and calls `logs/` "gitignored except its schema" (the ledger is tracked).
- **Visible clutter, little of it.** An empty `KGME_BB_RESTORATIONS/` at the top (left by the restore test of 2026-09-11) and a stray `docs/.Rhistory`. Caches, `.venv` and `.DS_Store` files are hidden and ignored.
