## What was built, by seam
- **The route.** `kgme session loose --handoff H --task T` fences a run to the task's Outputs from the record `kgme workspace create` wrote (`read_task_record`); the template renderer fills `{main}`, so the pinned list can name the main checkout's `kgme review`; `loose` runs get `BASH_DEFAULT_TIMEOUT_MS` and `BASH_MAX_TIMEOUT_MS` (25 and 30 minutes), since `make check` and a review cycle are one Bash call each and Claude Code's default is two minutes. `kgme review` accepts a KGME workspace (`--workspace`, or the working directory when it is one under `[crew] root`): the gate, the staged diff and the reviewer's working directory are the workspace's; the ledger, the audit, the evidence and the payloads stay the main checkout's; the patch and its hash are also written under the workspace's `logs/scratch/` so the prompt's relative paths hold. A review unit `HANDOFF-NNNN:<task>` keys a task's events; `:plan` and `:integration` are the crew's own. `kgme loop` takes a unit in its queue, with the adapter's argv and request, and closes a task on PASS plus a commit on its branch.
- **Workspaces** (`kgme/workspace.py`, `tests/test_workspace.py`). `create` makes the worktree under `[crew] root` on `crew/<handoff>/<task>` from `crew/<handoff>/integration` (made from `main` when new), syncs its environment, and writes `logs/scratch/workspaces/<name>/task.json` in the main checkout, never in the worktree, which the sandbox lets the builder's Bash write. `quarantine` kills the recorded process group (SIGTERM, SIGKILL after ten seconds), renames the branch `quarantine/<run>/<task>`, moves the worktree under `<root>/quarantine/`, strips write permission and appends one `blocking` outcome. `release` and `remove` refuse under an agent harness (the markers `kgme approve` reads).
- **The plan and its gate** (`kgme/crew.py`, `tests/test_crew_plan.py`). The plan is markdown the planner replies with; `check_plan` refuses a task without Outputs or an acceptance check, overlapping Outputs with no edge, a cycle, a human, blocklisted or Tier 1 path, an unknown harness, an edge to a task the plan does not name, and more independent tasks than `[crew] max_width`. `ready_tasks` follows the edges and holds a stopped task's dependents; `integration_order` is the plan's.
- **The crew** (`kgme/crew_io.py`, `tests/test_crew_run.py`). The planner is a fresh read-only `claude -p` on the `kgme-planner` skill; a red plan goes back with its refusals, a green one through `kgme.review_run.run_cycle` on the unit `:plan` with `control/review/plan-prompt.md`, by the other vendor's reviewer, with arbitration and the loop's halts; the loop stops on a plan seen before, a halt, or `[crew] max_plan_cycles`. Dispatch at `[crew] parallel` in plan order, each task in its workspace through `kgme loop` (Claude) or `run_codex_task` (Codex: build, the supervisor's gate and review on the staged diff, findings back to a fresh builder, the commit by pathspec on PASS). A halt, a stall or a merge-gate refusal quarantines; the rest integrate; the full gate and one `:integration` review by a vendor other than the majority of builders; then `awaiting approval` naming the head, and the screen.
- **The merge gate** (`crew.merge_gate`, `tests/test_merge_gate.py`; `crew_io.run_merge_gate`). Changed paths a subset of the Outputs; no human, blocklisted or Tier 1 path, symlink or binary; no zero-width or bidirectional control (fourteen code points, named); a dependency file without `docs/toolbox.md`; the scanner (`[crew] scanner`, else eight built-in rules naming the shape, never the value); `make check` and the task's acceptance check in a shared trial clone merging the branch on the integration head; green, `crew/<handoff>/integration` is moved to the trial's merge commit with `update-ref` and its old value, so a parallel landing is refused.
- **Approval** (`kgme/approve.py`). `--what code --sha <sha>` refuses a sha that is not a `crew/<handoff>/integration` head the crew wrote `awaiting approval` for; it appends the approval and fast-forwards `main` at a quiet point, then pushes through `kgme sync`.
- **Adapters** (`kgme/adapters/`, `tests/test_adapters.py`). One interface: `argv`, `request`, `start`, `events`, `kill`. Claude: `kgme session loose --handoff --task` from the workspace, hooks ledgering every call under the loop's parent (HANDOFF-0063). - **Skills** (`kgme/skills.py`). `kgme skills install` (Michou's) links each `skills/<name>/` into the two home folders and the two local ones, refuses to overwrite a skill it did not install, never touches `synced/`, copies on `--copy DIR` with a hash marker that `kgme lint` checks (`skills.read_findings`), and records the measured state of `~/.claude/skills/` in its note.
